Alongside applied policing research, our work relies heavily on classic database systems and computer security literature. Bell and LaPadula's 1973 model provided the theoretical foundation for our four-tier classification and clearance hierarchy. The normalization methodology in Elmasri and Navathe's Database Systems (2015) guided our functional dependency breakdown into Boyce-Codd Normal Form. In addition, the transaction isolation, logging, and concurrency principles from Silberschatz et al. (2020) informed our stored procedure design, savepoint rollbacks, and trigger-based audit logging.
